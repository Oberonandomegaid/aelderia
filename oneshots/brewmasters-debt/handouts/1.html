The contract

Folded small inside the cover of a brewer’s ledger. The ink is the red of old wine, and when no one is looking directly at it, the letters seem to move.

Agreement between Orrik Sallowbrand, Brewmaster of Kettlecairn, and the undersigned.

  1. The Undersigned shall restore Maudra Sallowbrand to full and lasting health.
  2. In return, the Brewmaster shall add the Ingredient provided to his festival stout, Lantern Dark, to be served upon the Night of Lanterns.
  3. Each person who drinks it shall owe one sip of soul to the Undersigned. No fiend shall take more than one sip from any one drinker.
  4. This agreement may not be undone.

Signed: Orrik Sallowbrand

Signed: a mark like a wine stain, shaped like a smiling mouth
