Pell Ambry’s pattern card: thinkstone ear-danglers

One pair: two chips, silver wire, steady hands. Do not breathe on the stones.

  1. Prepare the stones. Read the grain. Wake them gently (Arcana helps).
  2. Form the setting. Bend the wire into the cage (jeweller’s or tinker’s tools).
  3. Set and engrave. Seat each stone and mark the thinking-rune (tools again).
  4. Seal and bind. Close the cage and set the magic (spellcraft, or a clear head).

Two of you to a pair. Add your four efforts together; you need a strong total. If a stone cracks, there is more in the quarry vein. If both crack, I warned you.

— P. Ambry. PS: do not let the troll help.
