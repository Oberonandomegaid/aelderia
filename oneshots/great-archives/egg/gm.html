The Genesis Egg: GM notes

How it runs: the players scan the QR code and see the egg on the professor's desk. You play the professor, talk names with them, and when they agree one, they type it under Speak its name. Nothing on their screen ever lists the names.

Bonding

Buttons: Stroke, Nuzzle, Nudge, Feed, Beak and Say. Players can also stroke the bird with a finger (drag across it) or tap it: tap its beak, its head, or its tummy. It idles happily on its own and never moves the story on by itself.

Say lets the players talk to it. Kind words, its name or a hello make it happy; "come here", "jump" or "dance" get it bouncing; mention food, feathers, toys, books, shiny things, water or blankets and it reacts to each. Anything else gets a curious head tilt.

The bookcase

When you're ready, tell the players to tap Say and type bookcase. The bird flutters to a shelf… and the Archives' guardian punches it. BOING! SPLAT! The screen stops on the fist and a few drifting feathers. No fire, no rebirth on screen: the phoenix is yours to reveal at the table.

If someone refreshes the page afterwards, it stays on the aftermath.

Your hidden controls

Tap the top-left corner of the screen five times quickly. You can reset to the egg, hatch again, go back to the hatchling, replay the bookcase, or turn the sound on and off. Opening the page with ?gm on the end of the address opens them straight away.

Sound starts after the first tap (phones insist). The speaker button top right mutes it, and everything still works in silence.
