The coroner’s report

To Lord Xanthos of Silverwood, from Ysolde Marrable, Coroner of Fennick Cross.

My lord. Seven of the dead from Wendle Staithe have been brought to me. I set down what I have found.

  1. The skin is dry and unbroken. No wound, no puncture, no stretching or tearing. The bodies have not been opened.
  2. The insides are empty. Blood, fat, muscle, organs, sinew: all gone. What remains is smooth, as if polished.
  3. I cannot say when they died. They are so dry that a body a week dead looks like one dead a season.
  4. There is no residue of any kind. No stain, no powder, no smell beyond the ordinary.

I know of no disease, poison, beast or spell that does this.

One is tempted to wonder whether even the soul was taken. I set that down only because I cannot stop thinking it.

Y. Marrable
