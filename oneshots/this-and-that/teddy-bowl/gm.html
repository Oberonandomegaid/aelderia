Mr Cuddlesworth’s Bowl: GM quick reference

How it works: each searcher rolls a d20 and keeps the natural number. Read the row and tell the player the exact name. They type it into Mr Cuddlesworth’s Bowl. After four, they dunk the teddy and see what he becomes.
The Perfect Hug: if all four are the “1 or 20” results (Perfect Fluff, Honey Kiss, Lavender Crown, Heartstring Ribbon), the love overwhelms the ghost’s spite. Mr Cuddlesworth is gentle and there is no fight.
Base Mr Cuddlesworth: AC 13, HP 55, Speed 25 ft, two Padded Paws +5 (1d8+3), Big Cuddles grapple (escape DC 13), Crushing Affection 1d6. Add the bonuses below.
