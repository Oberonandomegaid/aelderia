<p data-source="AA-B0011">His family has recently purchased him a beautiful apothecary, hoping that the responsibility of operating a respectable business will encourage him to settle down.</p>



<p data-source="AA-B0012">They are mistaken.</p>



</div><div class="paragraph-group" data-audience="GM"><p data-source="AA-B0013">For several weeks, Zoltan has been developing experimental compounds, constructing strange machinery and making observations that he considers revolutionary.</p>



<p data-source="AA-B0014">Today, his family is coming to inspect their investment.</p>



<p data-source="AA-B0015">Zoltan has hired a group of adventurers to help finish some important experiments before they arrive.</p>



<p data-source="AA-B0016">Unfortunately, his instructions are ambiguous, his enthusiasm is overwhelming, and several of his creations are considerably less harmless than intended.</p>

I'VE WORKED IT OUT!"</p></blockquote><p data-source="AA-B0353" data-audience="GM">At that moment, an expensive carriage arrives.</p></div></div></div><div class="folio-footer"><span>This and That, That and This!</span><span>27</span></div>
