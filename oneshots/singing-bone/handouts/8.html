The invitation to the masquerade

By invitation of Lord Vale,

The honoured houses and friends of Duskwatch are called to an evening of reconciliation at the manor. A performance of exquisite new music will be offered when the bells mark nightfall.

Admission by seal or recognised household invitation. Servants, tradesmen and performers should present themselves at the garden delivery gate.

May all old discord find its resting place.

THE SINGING BONE · OBERON & OMEGA
