<h3>What's really going on</h3>
<ul>
<li><strong>Blacktide Bastion</strong> is a prison older than THK, built when nearly everyone had magic, and it looks exactly like what it is. It is a <strong>massive floating fortress</strong> with raw rock beneath it. Crowning it is the <strong>high-security wing</strong>: a great domed rotunda on columns, open to the sky between them. Smaller sections float either side, each linked to the main block by <strong>enormous chains</strong>.</li>
<li><strong>How it stays up.</strong> Ancient levitation magic keeps every section afloat. <strong>Anchor rods</strong>, set deep in the rock and working like great immovable rods, hold each section fixed in the sky. Without them, a strong wind would drift the whole prison away. The chains and rods were protected against rust and age by their builders, and still are.</li>
<li><strong>The defences.</strong> Statues line every wall and ledge, each built to strike with a different kind of magic, so that nothing is resisted. Anything that comes too close, they shoot. <strong>The only safe way in or out is the transport platform</strong>, which floats between the prison and Port Bastion. Even on the platform, an arm stuck out too far gets shot.</li>
<li><strong>Who runs it.</strong> Nobody living built it. A few hundred years ago, an earlier Lord Xanthos found it and brought its lost platform down to Port Bastion. Under the THK dynasty, its staff took binding contracts and truth-spells, and there was never any trouble. <strong>Since the Free Cities won their independence, about fifty years ago, the Free Cities have run it.</strong> The staff have changed, and the prison runs on trust. Mostly that works. A few have been bought.</li>
</ul></div><div class="flow-col"><ul>
<li><strong>Captain Zell</strong> commands four pirate ships. Lord Xanthos caught her three officers one by one and locked them here. Zell has <strong>arranged her own capture</strong> to break them out. Her ship, “impounded” in Port Bastion, is secretly being made ready to sail.</li>
<li><strong>A Trixie witch.</strong> Zell comes from a clan whose magic works through mist: illusions and conjured creatures. People call them <strong>Trixie witches</strong>. Alone she can make only a wisp of mist. <strong>Up among the clouds around the Bastion, she is formidable.</strong></li>
<li><strong>Xanthos</strong> has faced her twice, and she escaped both times. He finds this capture far too easy, and he says so.</li>
</ul>

<p><strong>Port Bastion</strong> is an ancient fortress cut into a mountainside above sheer cliffs and deep sea, its old ballistae and trebuchets still on the walls. It is a busy trading harbour now. Ships come in by two safe channels, left and right; between them lie sandbanks, rocks and wrecks.</p>
<p>High above the harbour floats <strong>Blacktide Bastion</strong>: a black stone fortress with rock hanging beneath it, its outlying sections chained to it, the domed high-security wing crowning it all. The <strong>transport platform</strong> floats up and down between the prison and the port, carrying guards, food and prisoners.</p>
<h3>The statues</h3>
<p>Every wall, tower and ledge of the Bastion carries <strong>statues</strong>, repeating all the way round. A great stone <strong>frog</strong> with its mouth open, that spits acid. A <strong>robed figure</strong> that hurls lightning. A <strong>lion</strong> that breathes fireballs. A <strong>many-handed figure</strong> that fills the air with magic missiles. Frost, thunder, poison: every kind of magic its builders could think of, so nothing can resist them all.</p>
<p><strong>Anything that comes too close, they shoot.</strong> The platform is the only exception, and only while you stay on it.</p>

<h3>Playing Lord Xanthos</h3>
<p>A <strong>leader first and a wizard second</strong>, far above the party in power, and <strong>very old</strong> now: white-haired, leaning on a cane, mind like a blade. Today he is <strong>talkative</strong>. He has worries, and he shares them. Ask his name: “<strong>Lord Xanthos.</strong> I have been Lord Xanthos for a very long time. It seems to get longer.”</p>
<ul>
<li><strong>Says he’s doing:</strong> seeing a dangerous pirate safely locked away.</li>
<li><strong>Privately:</strong> Zell was caught too easily. He wants capable people beside him, in case he is right.</li>
<li><strong>What he doesn’t know:</strong> the traitors, the ship, or how Trixie magic works.</li>
<li><strong>Once trapped:</strong> he can do nothing but talk. He doesn’t panic. He gives orders, and is rude about the traitors.</li>

<p>Lord Xanthos recruits the party through the <strong>Adventurers’ Guild in the capital</strong>. He pays <strong>very well: 500 gp each</strong>, half now. “<em>I am told you are capable. I may need capable people today. If I am wrong, then an old man has wasted his money. The air smells of salt and tar. And high above the harbour, so high that clouds drift beneath it, hangs a fortress: black stone, rock dangling from its underside like roots, smaller towers chained to it, all of it hanging in the sky as if someone had forgotten to let it fall.</em></p>
<p><em>Lord Xanthos leans on his cane and looks up at it with something like affection.</em></p>
<p><em>“Blacktide Bastion,” he says. “Older than anyone’s history. The finest prison ever built. Today it receives a guest.”</em></p></blockquote></div><div class="flow-col"><h3>Port Bastion</h3>
<p>On the way to the platform, sharp eyes may spot that <strong>Zell’s ship</strong>, the <strong>Grey Gull</strong>, lies in the impound berth, and its “caretakers” are loading water and stores for a ship that is supposedly going nowhere (DC 13 Perception or Insight). <p><strong>These clues don’t stop anything.</strong> They pay off in Act III.</p>

<p>Look out over the wing from the landing (the map, page 17). There are <strong>no corridors</strong>. <p>Three cells hold the officers, each collared: the <strong>far left</strong>, and the <strong>two straight ahead</strong> beyond the dais. <p>As the warden leads Zell across the dais to the empty cell on the right, Xanthos walking beside them, Zell turns her head, very slightly, towards Sergeant Morrow.</p><blockquote data-label="READ ALOUD"><p><em>Morrow lowers his hands, fingers spread.
