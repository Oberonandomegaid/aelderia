Restore the darkness

By the stars that shine so bright,
In the name of love and endless night,
With hearts united, let us stand,
Protect our haven, hand in hand.

In diversity, we find our might,
Embracing all, both day and night,
Eternal love, let it be known,
Each others hearts, are now our homes.

So let the cloak of darkness fall,
In unity, we heed the call,
With open hearts and courage strong,
Accepting all, we forever belong.

CRIMSON DAWN · OBERON & OMEGA
