Far below the docks, in a quarter the sea took long ago, a sentient bell has started to toll. Lord Xanthos brings six chosen adventurers to find out why. Down in the flooded city of the Nhalari, an ancient obelisk is failing, and something on the other side of it is waking up.</p><h3>What is really happening</h3><p>The obelisk is a lock on a door to another plane, where a horror of the deep the Nhalari call <strong>Nahál</strong> waits. Carved runes have held the door shut for centuries. They have worn away, and nobody remembers how to cut new ones. Lesser creatures are already slipping through. Tonight, at the full moon, the Rage of Nahál itself will push through the eye of the obelisk.</p></div><div><h3>Where it leads</h3><ul><li><strong>Act I:</strong> arrival, the bell, the Nhalari, and a first fight under water.</li><li><strong>Act II:</strong> three drowned storerooms, to recover the original carved rune fragments.</li><li><strong>Act III:</strong> the Rage of Nahál squeezes through the eye. <h3>What's really going on</h3>
<ul>
<li><strong>Royal Harbour sank slowly, not suddenly.</strong> It sits on an estuary of tremendous depth. For generations, people built new streets on top of old ones, and the old ones settled into the mud and water. Some lower levels are dry, some half-flooded, some lost entirely. No curse did this. Just time, and weight, and the sea.</li>
<li><strong>The Nhalari are the city’s oldest secret.</strong> Fourteen centuries ago, their seafaring ancestors were betrayed and enslaved here. They built their own quarter. As it sank, they adapted, generation by generation, until the sea itself kept their oppressors out. Now they are a free people below the docks, who trade fish and knotwork with the surface for iron and grain.</li>
<li><strong>They brought their gods with them.</strong> Among them, a horror of the deep they honour the way sailors honour a storm: not out of love, but in hope of mercy. Its manifested fury is the <strong>Rage of Nahál</strong>.</li>
<li><strong>The obelisk is a lock.</strong> It holds a door to Nahál’s realm. The crystal inside anchors the connection; the runes carved on the outside stop it opening both ways. The Nhalari feed it living fish at every full-moon tide, but the offerings were never what held it. The runes were, and the runes have worn away.</li>

<div class="running" style="--accent:#1f4f5a"><span>The Drowned Bell</span><span>GM</span></div><div class="pf-body columns" style="--accent:#1f4f5a"><figure class="tb-banner" style="height:160px"><img alt="" src="https://oberonandomegaid.github.io/aelderia/oneshots/drowned-bell/img/banner-setting.jpg" style="object-position:50% 50%"></figure><div class="layout-cols"><div class="flow-col"><h3>The setting</h3>
<p>Above the water, <strong>Royal Harbour</strong> is a busy royal port: quays, warehouses, a cathedral of grey stone, ships from everywhere. Below the quays, it goes on downwards. The cellars have cellars. The oldest streets lie half under the tide, black water lapping at doorways carved fourteen centuries ago. Below those, the drowned quarter of the <strong>Nhalari</strong>, lit by glowing kelp and pale fish, where the streets are swum, not walked.</p>
<p>And in the deepest part, in a hall of carved stone, stands <strong>the obelisk</strong>: tall as three men, black, ringed with worn carvings, with a single round hole bored through near the top, like an eye.</p>
<p>Above it, hanging in a sunken tower, is <strong>the Old Voice</strong>.</p>
<h3>The Nhalari</h3>
<p>Nearly translucent skin, so you can see the shadow of bone beneath; slightly webbed hands and feet; eyes that catch light like a cat’s. They swim better than anything on two legs. Among themselves they speak in clicks of tongue and teeth, deep throat resonance and gulps of water; their Common is good but carries those sounds with it. They work magic in <strong>knots</strong>: rope and seaweed tied and stitched into symbols that bless ships, read the tides and remember. They fight with spears of bone and coral, sometimes tipped with fish venom: quick and deadly, and easily hurt.</p></div><div class="flow-col"><h3>Under water: the rules</h3>
<p>This adventure uses the official 2024 rules for underwater combat, and nothing extra.</p>
<ul>
<li><strong>Melee weapons:</strong> a creature without a Swim Speed has Disadvantage on melee weapon attacks, unless the weapon deals Piercing damage (spears, daggers, tridents, rapiers).</li>
<li><strong>Ranged weapons:</strong> attacks beyond normal range automatically miss; attacks within normal range have Disadvantage.</li>
<li><strong>Fire:</strong> anything underwater has Resistance to Fire damage. Fire spells still work.</li>
<li><strong>Lightning and thunder</strong> behave exactly as written. They don’t spread through the water.</li>
<li><strong>Breathing:</strong> every character has <strong>two Potions of Water Breathing</strong> from Lord Xanthos (1 hour each). Don’t count breaths.</li>
<li><strong>Swimming</strong> costs 1 extra foot per foot of movement, unless you have a Swim Speed.</li>
</ul>

<div class="running" style="--accent:#1f4f5a"><span>The Drowned Bell</span><span>GM</span></div><div class="pf-body columns" style="--accent:#1f4f5a"><figure class="tb-banner" style="height:130px"><img alt="" src="https://oberonandomegaid.github.io/aelderia/oneshots/drowned-bell/img/banner-act1.jpg" style="object-position:50% 50%"></figure><div class="layout-cols"><div class="flow-col"><h2>Act I — Arrival and First Blood</h2>
<p><em>About 45 minutes.</em></p>
<h3>The bell</h3><blockquote data-label="READ ALOUD"><p><em>You hear it before you see the harbour: a deep, slow note that comes up through the soles of your boots rather than through the air. Then another. Then another.</em></p>
<p><em>Every dog on the quayside is howling. Every face on the street is turned towards the water.</em></p>
<p><em>Beside you, Lord Xanthos does not slow down. “That,” he says, “is the reason you are here.”</em></p></blockquote><p><strong>The Old Voice</strong> tolls every few minutes, day and night. Everyone in the city hears it. Every character who hears it for the first time feels a cold, rising dread they can’t explain, the sense that something is coming, and they are late. It does no harm. It means it.</p>
<h3>Why Lord Xanthos is here</h3>
<p>His instruments detected a planar disturbance under Royal Harbour. He knows the Nhalari: he has traded with them for years, and admires their knot-runes enough to have learned a few. He chose the party himself. He doesn’t yet know what is down there.</p>
<p>If asked his name: “<strong>Lord Xanthos.</strong> You were told whom you were working for, I trust.”</p></div><div class="flow-col"><figure class="tb-pic"><div class="tb-frame" style="height:350px"><img alt="Lord Xanthos, 1060 THK." src="https://oberonandomegaid.github.io/aelderia/oneshots/drowned-bell/img/xanthos-portrait.jpg" style="object-position:50% 0%"></div><figcaption>Lord Xanthos, 1060 THK.</figcaption></figure><h3>Playing Lord Xanthos</h3>
<p>A <strong>leader first and a wizard second</strong>, five or six levels above the party: confident, aristocratic, and entirely uninterested in arguing with people who know less than he does. He is genuinely respectful of the Nhalari, in his own way, and genuinely impatient with everyone else.</p>
<ul>
<li><strong>Says he’s doing:</strong> preventing a breach under a royal city.</li>
<li><strong>Privately:</strong> fascinated by a prison that has held for fourteen centuries.</li>
<li><strong>In this adventure:</strong> he diagnoses, then holds the breach. He never goes into the storerooms and never fights the Rage for them.</li>
</ul></div></div></div><div class="folio-footer"><span>The Drowned Bell</span><span>5</span></div>

<div class="running" style="--accent:#1f4f5a"><span>The Drowned Bell</span><span>GM</span></div><div class="pf-body columns" style="--accent:#1f4f5a"><div class="layout-cols"><div class="flow-col"><h3>Down to the drowned quarter</h3>
<p>Harbourmaster <strong>Bede Calloway</strong>, sweating through his coat, takes the party down through warehouse cellars to the tide-line, where old stone steps run down into black water. A Nhalari escort waits there, faces just breaking the surface.</p><blockquote data-label="READ ALOUD"><p><em>They rise out of the water without a ripple. Pale, almost glass-clear skin, the shadow of bone beneath. Eyes like a cat’s at night. One of them clicks her tongue twice and speaks, in Common thick with the sea.</em></p>
<p><em>“Xanthos-lord. Ktk. You come because the Old Voice calls. Good. Bring your people. Drink your little bottles. Follow.”</em></p></blockquote><h3>Elder Ullu-Ktah</h3>
<p>In the drowned quarter, a hall of knotted seaweed and carved stone, the party meet <strong>Elder Ullu-Ktah</strong>, the knot-mage elder: tiny, ancient, swathed in rope tied into thousands of symbols. She is proud, wary of surface folk, and far from sure that handing the obelisk to outsiders is wise.</p>
<p>Lord Xanthos wants to examine the obelisk at once. She will not allow it. He will not debate it. <strong>The party have to bridge that gap.</strong></p></div><div class="flow-col"><figure class="tb-pic"><div class="tb-frame" style="height:230px"><img alt="Elder Ullu-Ktah and her people." src="https://oberonandomegaid.github.io/aelderia/oneshots/drowned-bell/img/nhalari.jpg" style="object-position:50% 25%"></div><figcaption>Elder Ullu-Ktah and her people.</figcaption></figure><div class="gm-note"><h4>Winning the elder round</h4><p>Any of these works. Let the players find their own.</p>
<ul>
<li><strong>Respect.</strong> Ask permission properly, learn a greeting, accept the knotted blessing she offers. No roll needed.</li>
<li><strong>Honesty.</strong> Tell her what the bell means to the surface (DC 12 Insight shows she already knows; she wanted to see if they would say it).</li>
<li><strong>Persuasion.</strong> DC 14 Charisma; Advantage if anyone has shown respect first.</li>
<li><strong>The fight that comes next.</strong> If they save Ollo, she trusts them.</li>
</ul></div><p>Once she agrees, Lord Xanthos examines the obelisk in silence for a long time, then tells everyone the truth (see page 9).</p></div></div></div><div class="folio-footer"><span>The Drowned Bell</span><span>6</span></div>

<div class="running" style="--accent:#1f4f5a"><span>The Drowned Bell</span><span>GM</span></div><div class="pf-body columns" style="--accent:#1f4f5a"><div class="layout-cols"><div class="flow-col"><h3>First blood</h3>
<p>On the way back to the obelisk hall, the water goes cloudy. A <strong>giant octopus</strong>, already bleeding, comes tumbling through the drowned street, with <strong>Sesh and Ollo</strong> right behind it, spears ready. It turns on Ollo and gets a tentacle round him. The blood in the water has drawn <strong>4 reef sharks</strong> from the deeps.</p><figure class="tb-banner" style="height:180px;margin:6px 0 8px"><img alt="Sesh and Ollo and the wounded creature" src="https://oberonandomegaid.github.io/aelderia/oneshots/drowned-bell/img/firstblood.jpg" style="object-position:50% 45%"></figure><p>This fight teaches the underwater rules. It should be forgiving: about 600 XP, well under Low for this party. <strong>The octopus starts at half its Hit Points</strong> (22) and is already slowed by its wound.</p></div><div class="flow-col"><div class="combat-note"><h4>Encounter · GM reference</h4><p><strong>Terrain.</strong> A drowned street 20 ft. wide between sunken houses, open water above, a doorway or two to duck into. Everyone is swimming.</p>
<p><strong>Ollo</strong> is Grappled and Restrained by the octopus, at 4 Hit Points. Without help he drops next round. <strong>Sesh</strong> fights beside the party (use the Nhalari Spear-Warrior). She is fast and brave and goes down in two hits.</p>
<p><strong>Teach as you go.</strong> The first time someone swings a sword: Disadvantage, because it isn’t Piercing. The first time someone fires a bow: say whether the target is within normal range. The first fire spell: Resistance, but it works.</p>
<p><strong>When it turns.</strong> The sharks flee when two are dead. The octopus uses Ink Cloud and bolts if it drops below 10 Hit Points.</p></div><h3>Ollo</h3>

<div class="running" style="--accent:#1f4f5a"><span>The Drowned Bell</span><span>GM</span></div><div class="pf-body columns" style="--accent:#1f4f5a"><div class="layout-cols"><div class="flow-col"><h3>The five seals</h3>
<p>Lord Xanthos’s snap won’t hold long. Now the obelisk has to be sealed properly: <strong>five seals</strong>, each tied in knot-runes copied from the fragments, while the knot-mages bind them into the stone.</p>
<p><strong>On phones:</strong> the players scan the QR code on the handout page (page 27). The page shows five seals. <strong>Assign each player a seal.</strong> When you start the timer, they press <em>Show me the knot</em>, watch a pattern of knot-runes light up and chime, then tap them back in the same order.</p>
<p><strong>Without phones:</strong> use the printed backup (opposite).</p><div class="gm-note"><h4>How a round works</h4><ol>
<li><strong>Start a 60-second timer.</strong> Everyone working on a seal tries at once.</li>
<li><strong>A slip isn’t the end:</strong> a wrong tap gives a new knot to try, if there is still time.</li>
<li><strong>A finished seal stays finished.</strong> Tick it on your sheet. It can never be undone.</li>
<li><strong>At the end of the timer: one Spawn of Nahál comes through for every seal still unfinished.</strong> Three seals done? Two spawn.</li>
<li><strong>Fight them.</strong> Lord Xanthos holds the breach meanwhile.</li>
<li><strong>Then another 60 seconds</strong>, for the seals still open. Players may swap seals, help each other, or let a different player try. Repeat until all five are done.</li>
</ol></div></div><div class="flow-col"><h3>Keeping it fair</h3>
<ul>
<li>The knots get longer: seals I and II are four runes, III and IV five, V six. Give the hardest to whoever wants it.</li>
<li>Spawn are weak on purpose: a nuisance, not a second boss. If the party are almost dead after the Rage, let Lord Xanthos blast one spawn a round.</li>
<li><strong>Accessibility:</strong> a player who can’t do the memory game can describe a plan to Elder Ullu-Ktah instead, with a DC 13 Intelligence (Arcana) or Wisdom check per seal, in the same 60 seconds.</li>
</ul>
<h3>Printed backup</h3>
<p>Show the player a knot card from the appendix for <strong>five seconds</strong>, then hide it. They point to the runes in order on the ring sheet. Same timer, same spawn.</p>
