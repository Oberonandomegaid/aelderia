Tobiah’s notes

A sheaf of damp pages, written in a neat, careful hand that grows shakier towards the end.

Notes on the Saltgrave. T. Fennimore.

Day 1. Boarded at dusk. A delightful crew. They have offered me a drink six times. I have accepted twice.

Day 1, later. I cannot leave. I tried the gangplank. I am standing on the deck again. Most unsettling.

The pearl. The captain calls it the Heart. Black, fist-sized, warm to look at. Kept in a whalebone coffer in his cabin. Key lost below. It binds the dead, and holds the ship together. I came to bring it home. I now believe it cannot be carried. It must be broken.

The hold. Flooded with another sea. Something large moves in it. It ignores the crew.

Tonight I will try for the key.

The last line is smudged, as if written in a hurry, or in fear:

If anyone reads this, tell X I am sorry.

THE THIRTEENTH TIDE · OBERON & OMEGA
