The bard's last letter

To Thaloran, my beloved,

They would have me confess to treason. You know the song I sang and why I sang it. I turned the invaders back, and my lord feared the mouths that called it courage.

If they still silence me, let the last thing made of me be music, not a weapon. If you can bear it, take these bones and make one final song. Let it tell the truth when my voice is gone.

Do not let them teach the world that I betrayed it. All my love, until the final note.

THE SINGING BONE · OBERON & OMEGA
