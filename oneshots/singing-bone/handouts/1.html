Vannor's coded introduction

Master Penmoor,

The bearers come with my trust, though I cannot grant it to them in your name. They know of our old friend's winter journey and of the debt that followed. Hear them out in private; offer no names in the street.

If the daughter yet has shelter, see that the strangers first understand what a spoken truth can cost in Veldenhollow. There is an herb merchant who will know where that lesson may be found.

— E. Vannor

THE SINGING BONE · OBERON & OMEGA
