<h3>What's really going on</h3>
<ul>
<li><strong>Blacktide Bastion</strong> is a prison older than THK, built when nearly everyone had magic. It floats high above the sea on old levitation magic, held in place by <strong>enormous chains</strong>. Its upper wing has <strong>doorless cells</strong> sealed by force barriers, and <strong>anti-magic collars</strong> for every prisoner. Nobody living built it. Years ago, Lord Xanthos found its lost transport platform and brought it back into use, but <strong>he doesn’t run the prison</strong>. The Free Cities do.</li>
<li><strong>The weak spot.</strong> Under the old dynasties, prison staff took binding oaths and truth-spells. The Free Cities trust their staff instead. Mostly that works. A few have been bought.</li>
<li><strong>Captain Zell</strong> commands a fleet of four pirate ships. Lord Xanthos caught her three officers one by one and locked them here. Zell has <strong>arranged her own capture</strong> to break them out. Her ship, “impounded” in Port Bastion, is secretly being made ready to sail.</li>
<li><strong>Her magic.</strong> Zell’s people come from a misty swamp, and their magic works through mist: illusions and conjured creatures. Alone she can make only a wisp. <strong>Up among the clouds around the Bastion, she is formidable.</strong></li>
<li><strong>Xanthos</strong> has caught her before, and lost her. He finds this capture far too easy, and says so.</li>

<p><strong>Port Bastion</strong> is an ancient fortress cut into a mountainside above sheer cliffs and deep sea, its old ballistae and trebuchets still on the walls. It is a busy trading harbour now. Ships come in by two safe channels, left and right; between them lie sandbanks, rocks and wrecks.</p>
<p>High above the harbour hangs <strong>Blacktide Bastion</strong>: a black stone fortress floating in the clouds, held by four great chains anchored in the mountain. A single <strong>transport platform</strong> climbs and descends one of the chains, carrying guards, food and prisoners.</p>
<p><strong>The aerial defences.</strong> Bronze watchers on the prison’s walls shoot down anything that flies close without permission. They <strong>don’t fire at anything touching the chains</strong>, so that maintenance crews can work.</p>

<h3>Playing Lord Xanthos</h3>
<p>A <strong>leader first and a wizard second</strong>, far above the party in power, and <strong>very old</strong> now: white-haired, slow on stairs, mind like a blade. Measured, courteous, dry. Ask his name: “<strong>Lord Xanthos.</strong> I have been Lord Xanthos for a very long time. It seems to get longer.”</p>
<ul>
<li><strong>Says he’s doing:</strong> seeing a dangerous pirate safely locked away.</li>
<li><strong>Privately:</strong> Zell was caught too easily. He wants witnesses he can trust.</li>
<li><strong>What he doesn’t know:</strong> the traitors, the ship, or how her mist magic works.</li>
<li><strong>Once trapped:</strong> he can do nothing but talk. He doesn’t panic. He gives advice, and is rude about the traitors.</li>

<p><em>About 50 minutes.</em></p>
<h3>The hire</h3>
<p>Lord Xanthos recruits the party through the <strong>Adventurers’ Guild in the capital</strong>, <strong>200 gp each</strong> “to stand about looking capable,” and opens a portal straight to Port Bastion.</p><blockquote data-label="READ ALOUD"><p><em>You step through onto a harbour wall above a grey-green sea. A royal warship is easing in through the left-hand channel, and on her deck, in chains, stands a woman in a sea-stained coat, smiling.</em></p>
<p><em>Lord Xanthos watches her with his hands folded on his cane.</em></p>
<p><em>“Captain Zell,” he says. “I have caught her once before. She did not stay caught.” He glances at you. “And this time she was very easy to catch. I find that I do not like it.”</em></p></blockquote></div><div class="flow-col"><h3>Port Bastion</h3>
<p>Let the party look around while the prisoner is unloaded. Clues for sharp eyes:</p>
<ul>
<li><strong>Zell’s ship</strong>, the <strong>Grey Gull</strong>, lies in the impound berth. Its “caretakers” are busy loading water and stores for a ship that is supposedly going nowhere (DC 13 Perception or Insight).</li>
<li>A harbour official counts a fat purse and looks away (DC 14 Insight).</li>
<li><strong>Mirella</strong>, an old fortune-teller on the quay, mutters: “<em>Mist-woman. Swamp-born. Doesn’t need much. Up in the clouds she’ll have plenty.</em>” (Optional: see Characters.)</li>
</ul>
<p><strong>These clues don’t stop anything.</strong> They pay off in Act III.</p>
<h3>Zell herself</h3>
<p>If anyone talks to her: she is charming, mocking and unworried. “<em>Lord Xanthos. Still alive? How marvellous. We must stop meeting like this.</em>” She asks the party’s names, and remembers them.</p></div></div></div><div class="folio-footer"><span>The Prison Without Doors</span><span>5</span></div>

<p>On the way up, Xanthos tells the party about Zell’s three officers, held in the wing above. <strong>Make sure the players hear the weapons.</strong> They will need to recognise them.</p><blockquote data-label="READ ALOUD"><p><em>“The Iron Butcher. <p><em>“The Stormcaller. Fast. Hits like weather. A curved greatsword that cracks like thunder when it lands.</em></p>
<p><em>“I took those weapons from them myself. They are in the prison’s armoury now, where they belong.”</em></p></blockquote><p><strong>They aren’t.</strong> The traitors stole them weeks ago.</p></div></div></div><div class="folio-footer"><span>The Prison Without Doors</span><span>6</span></div>

<div class="running" style="--accent:#3e4a5c"><span>The Prison Without Doors</span><span>GM</span></div><div class="pf-body columns" style="--accent:#3e4a5c"><div class="layout-cols"><div class="flow-col"><h3>The high-security wing</h3>
<p>The platform docks at the top of the prison, in the clouds. A <strong>landing</strong>, a <strong>stairwell</strong>, and beyond them the <strong>transfer hall</strong>: a round chamber open to the sky on one side, where cloud drifts in. Three corridors lead off it to the three <strong>officers’ cells</strong>. In the middle is the empty cell waiting for Zell. <strong>No door</strong>: just an archway that shimmers when the barrier is up.</p>
<p>The warden asks the party to <strong>wait by the landing and stairwell</strong>, with a clear view of the transfer. Xanthos goes with Zell to the cell.</p>
<h3>The signal</h3><blockquote data-label="READ ALOUD"><p><em>At the cell, the warden raises her token to drop the barrier. Captain Zell turns her head, very slightly, towards Sergeant Morrow.</em></p>
<p><em>Morrow lowers his hands, fingers spread. He bows his head. One knee bends, just a little. Around the hall, three other guards do exactly the same.</em></p></blockquote><p>A DC 15 Insight check notices the gesture: hands down, fingers spread, head bowed, knee bent. It’s the <strong>sign of Zell’s people</strong>. Anyone who sees it can <strong>tell traitors from loyal guards</strong> for the rest of the adventure.</p></div><div class="flow-col"><h3>The betrayal</h3><blockquote data-label="READ ALOUD"><p><em>It happens in a single breath. Morrow’s hand flicks out with a key, and Zell’s collar falls away. Another guard snaps it shut around Lord Xanthos’s throat. A third shoves the old man backwards, through the archway, into the cell.</em></p>
<p><em>The barrier flares up behind him, blue and humming.</em></p>
<p><em>And Captain Zell breathes in the cloud drifting through the hall as if it were sea air, and laughs, and the whole chamber fills with mist.</em></p></blockquote><p><strong>Zell</strong> shouts orders through the mist: “<em>Morrow, the officers! The rest of you, keep these off me!</em>” Gulls made of mist burst from the fog around her, and she is gone, into the prison, heading down.</p><blockquote data-label="READ ALOUD"><p><em>From inside the cell, Lord Xanthos’s voice, sharp and clear:</em></p>
<p><em>“Do not let them arm the officers. Three guards, three corridors. They have bags. Stop them, or chase her. Choose quickly.”</em></p></blockquote></div></div></div><div class="folio-footer"><span>The Prison Without Doors</span><span>7</span></div>

<p><em>About 70 minutes.</em></p>
<h3>The runners</h3>

<li><strong>Zell has a Mist Double up?</strong> He casts <em>Dispel Magic</em> on it, or simply says which is the real one.</li>
<li><strong>An officer is beating someone badly?</strong> <em>Hold Person</em> (DC 19) on that officer.</li>
<li><strong>Otherwise</strong>, a cantrip at the nearest officer (<em>Fire Bolt</em>, +11, 3d10).</li>
</ol>
<h3>What he never does</h3>
<ul>
<li><strong>Kill Zell</strong>, or land the blow that ends an officer.</li>
<li><strong>Chase Zell</strong> himself: he is old, slow, and holding the <em>Wall of Force</em>.</li>
<li><strong>Counter a player’s spell</strong>, or take a character’s turn.</li>
</ul>
