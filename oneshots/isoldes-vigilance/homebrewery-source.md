<p><strong>The one promise this adventure makes:</strong> the legend is fixed. Isolde becomes Vigilance, and Roderick kills the red dragon threatening Emberholme. Everything the Order does to save the village, and the fate of its other members, is resolved through play.</p>

</blockquote><h4 data-source="IV-B0006" data-audience="GM" id="iv-b0006">The canon you are protecting</h4><p data-source="IV-B0007" data-audience="GM">In Eldoria, Isolde and her husband Roderick serve Veyrath, Witness of the Given Word, within the Order of the Crimson Light. They confront a red dragon devastating Emberholme. The Order is overwhelmed; Isolde suffers a mortal wound protecting her husband. Veyrath offers her continued service in another form. She accepts and becomes the sentient greatsword Vigilance. Roderick slays the dragon and later raises a monument to the fallen, placing Vigilance within it. This is the origin already established in <em>Shadow over Silverwood</em>.</p><h4 data-source="IV-B0009" data-audience="GM" id="iv-b0009">The GM's pre-game conversation (read or paraphrase)</h4><blockquote data-source="IV-B0010" class="read-aloud" data-label="OPTIONAL VOICE / READ ALOUD" data-audience="GM">

<p>“This is a historical tragedy. Isolde will give her life in a divine transformation, and Roderick will kill the dragon with the sword she becomes. You're playing how the Order gets there. Other heroes can survive or die; the village can be saved or devastated. Your choices change the cost and meaning of the legend, not those two established events. We're playing dragon hunters who knowingly face an enemy beyond their preparation.”</p>

</blockquote></div><div class="flow-col"><p data-source="IV-B0011" data-audience="GM">Ask the Isolde and Roderick players explicitly whether they want these roles. If someone doesn't, reassign the role before starting. Warn the group about fantasy combat, burning buildings, corrosive injury, grief and heroic death; use ordinary table safety boundaries, and fade out any description that a player does not want.</p><h4 data-source="IV-B0012" data-audience="GM" id="iv-b0012">Rules assumptions and practical setup</h4><p data-source="IV-B0013" data-audience="GM">This adventure uses the 2024 fifth-edition rules (SRD 5.2.1), with a few adventure-specific abilities that are clearly marked. Isolde and Roderick are required; fill the other seats with the Order (Talen first, then Dorrin or Mira, then Seren) or with any of the level 10 pre-generated characters at the back of this book. The dragon’s hit point tracks scale with the actual number of PCs.</p><p data-source="IV-B0014" data-audience="GM">Prepare: the six PC sheets; a <strong>single replacement VIGILANCE ASCENDANT stat-block card</strong> kept out of sight until the transformation; two red-wyrmling markers; one adult-dragon marker; a 1-inch-grid village square map (60 by 60 feet or larger, with surrounding streets); a 1-inch-grid ravine map; six village-preparation boxes; two 300 gp diamonds in Talen's equipment; and a note of spell slots spent. Roderick's baseline and transformed attacks are already calculated on his sheet and on Vigilance's card.</p><p data-source="IV-B0015" data-audience="GM">If short on time, cut the road wagon conversation and shorten village exploration; <strong>do not cut</strong> the wyrmling fight, the warding choice, Isolde's new card, or the memorial.</p><h2 data-source="IV-B0016" data-audience="GM" id="iv-b0016">ACT I — THOSE WHO HAVE GIVEN THEIR WORD</h2><p data-source="IV-B0017" data-audience="GM"><strong>Target runtime: 15 minutes</strong></p></div></div></div><div class="folio-footer"><span>Isolde’s Vigilance</span><span>3</span></div>

<p>“The Crimson Light? Thank the gods. The village of Emberholme is burning. Smaller dragons have been seen near the old quarry, but something enormous has come down from the high country. We need hunters — and we need them today.”</p>

</blockquote><p data-source="IV-B0022" data-audience="GM"><strong>Corven, Emberholme's courier</strong> is exhausted but sincere. </blockquote><p data-source="IV-B0028" data-audience="GM"><strong>Three village people who matter:</strong></p><p data-source="IV-B0029" data-audience="GM"><strong>Maera Fen, reeve.</strong> Sharp-eyed, sleeves rolled up. “I don't need vows.
