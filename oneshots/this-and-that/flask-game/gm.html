Zoltan’s Flask: GM quick reference

How it works: each searcher rolls a d20 and keeps the natural number. Read the row, then tell the player the exact name. They type it into Zoltan’s Flask on their phone. After six ingredients they pour, and their Flubb appears. All six rainbow = the Rainbow Flubb pet, no fight.
Base Flubb: AC 11, HP 24, Speed 30 ft, slam +4 for 1d6+2 bludgeoning. Add the ichor and dust damage to the slam, the heart HP, the skin AC, and both resistances.

Ichor: extra damage on its slam

d20Name to tell the playerEffectWhat players see
1 or 20Rainbow IchorNo bonus damageIts base puddles into harmless rainbow goo.
2–7Hard Ichor+1d4 bludgeoningIts base has set like grey stone.
8–13Spikey Ichor+1d4 piercingIts base bristles with green spikes.
14–19Sharp Ichor+1d4 slashingIts base is a pool of red, razor-edged shards.
