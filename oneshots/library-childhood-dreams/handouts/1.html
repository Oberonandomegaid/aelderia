Elsie’s page

A page torn from the Blank Book, found in Elsie’s nest. The writing is a child’s: big, careful letters, some of them backwards.

Once upon a time there was a girl called Elsie and she had a friend.

He was a bear called MISTER BRAMBLECOAT and he was the BIGGEST bear and he was knitted and he was never ever cross and he liked tag.

And Elsie had a mum and a dad who were a Queen and a King and they lived in a castle made of cake and nobody was ever sent to bed with no supper.

And nobody ever came to take them away.

The End.
