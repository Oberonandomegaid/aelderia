The transfer order

A transfer order, stamped and sealed, carried by Warden Quarne.

BLACKTIDE BASTION · TRANSFER OF PRISONER

Prisoner: “Captain Zell”, pirate, commander of four ships. Cell: High-security wing, transfer hall, central cell. Restraint: Anti-magic collar at all times. Do not remove at any point during transfer.

Note: The prisoner’s three officers are held in the same wing (north, east and south cells). Under no circumstances are they to be brought within sight of her.

Witnessed by: Lord Xanthos of Silverwood.

Guard detail: Sgt. D. Morrow and four.

In the margin, in a neat, old-fashioned hand:

Too easy. Watch her hands. And watch theirs. X.

THE PRISON WITHOUT DOORS · OBERON & OMEGA
