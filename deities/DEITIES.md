## Status
- All deity banners listed here have the founder's visual approval.
- The original eight remain established canon.
- The three newly added deities and their approved visual identities are additions to be incorporated into the manuscript when authorised.

## Rules for the team
- Never state how many deities there are (the number may change); say "one of the deities of Aelderia".
- Use the names, domains and emblems exactly as written here. Do not invent new gods, rename them or change their emblems; propose changes to Zeus.
- Art prompts that show a deity's symbol must match the emblem and banner above.
- Do not add a god to a project's manuscript without founder authorisation.
