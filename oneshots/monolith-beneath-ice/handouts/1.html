The talking stone

Drawn by my gran, Edda Thorley, the summer before they made the lake.

The Talking Stone. Stands in Cobb’s field, past the oak. Tall as three men. Black. A silver curl on its face like a snail shell. Never throw stones at it. They come back.

Gran said on still nights you could hear it talking. Not to you. To itself.

THE MONOLITH BENEATH THE ICE · OBERON & OMEGA
