The Warden’s journal

Year upon year I have sung them into the dark and closed the doors and kept the lamps. And year upon year they have lain there, alone, and nobody came.

I will not leave them. I have found the way back. Piran spoke to me today. He knew his name. He asked for his mother.

They weep sometimes. The flaw is in them, not the work. A little more, and they will be whole.

THE DEAD SHALL RISE · OBERON & OMEGA
