The Guild commission

To the Adventurers’ Guild,

House Valtair requests the services of a company of capable adventurers bearing no connection, of blood, service, debt or friendship, to the Valtair family.

Present yourselves at the Valtair house this afternoon, at Lady Octavia’s convenience, which is to say promptly.

Terms generous; payment through the Guild.

O. V.
