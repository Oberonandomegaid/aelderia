<div class="running" style="--accent:#2f5a5a"><span>The Thirteenth Tide</span><span>GM ONLY</span></div><div class="pf-body gm-synopsis" style="--accent:#2f5a5a"><h1>GM synopsis</h1><p class="synopsis-deck">The Thirteenth Tide</p><div class="synopsis-columns"><div><h3>The adventure</h3><p>The pirate ship <em>Saltgrave</em> vanished long ago. Every thirteen years she sails back out of another sea for a single night, crewed by ghosts and loaded with treasure. Thirteen years ago, Lord Xanthos sent his oldest colleague aboard to study her. He never came back. Now she has returned, and Xanthos has hired the party to find out what happened. He will not set foot on her himself.</p><h3>What is really happening</h3><p>A black pearl, the <em>Saltgrave’s</em> Heart, binds the soul of everyone who dies aboard and keeps the rotten old ship afloat. And once a living guest steps aboard, it will not let them leave. The only way off is to destroy the Heart. Then the souls go free, and the ship sinks.</p></div><div><h3>Where it leads</h3><ul><li><strong>Act I:</strong> a warm welcome, games and grog, and a gangplank that won’t let you go.</li><li><strong>Act II:</strong> a ghostly scholar, a shifting chart, a cheating quartermaster and something hungry in the flooded hold.</li><li><strong>Act III:</strong> the captain, a mutiny, the Heart destroyed, and a sinking ship full of gold.</li><li><strong>Ending:</strong> dawn, a farewell, and whatever the party could carry.</li></ul><p>Built for six level-5 characters in one session of about 3–3½ hours. 1467 THK.</p></div></div><section class="story-navigation"><h2>At the table</h2><div class="nav-grid"><div><h4>Story route</h4><p><a href="#p3">Running this adventure</a></p><p><a href="#p5">Act I · Welcome Aboard</a></p><p><a href="#p8">Act II · The Devil’s Own Treasure Hunt</a></p><p><a href="#p13">Act III · Mutiny on the Thirteenth Tide</a></p><p><a href="#p20">Ending · Dawn</a></p></div><div><h4>Reference</h4><p><a href="#p22">GM quick reference</a></p><p><a href="#p23">The crew</a></p><p><a href="#p18">Map</a></p><p><a href="#p24">Characters</a></p><p><a href="#p25">Player handout</a></p></div></div></section></div><div class="folio-footer"><span>The Thirteenth Tide</span><span>2</span></div>

<h3>What's really going on</h3>
<ul>
<li><strong>The Saltgrave.</strong> The most feared pirate ship of her age, under <strong>Captain Absalom Rook</strong>. On her last voyage she raided a drowned shrine and took a black pearl the size of a fist. Within a month, she vanished with all hands.</li>
<li><strong>The Heart.</strong> The pearl binds the soul of anyone who dies aboard, and keeps the ship afloat long after she should have rotted. She sails an <strong>interplanar sea</strong> now, and every <strong>thirteen years</strong> she rises into Aelderia’s waters for one night, from dusk to dawn.</li>
<li><strong>The trap.</strong> Any living creature who boards her <strong>cannot leave</strong>. Step back over the side and you are simply standing on her deck again. When she sails at dawn, guests sail with her. In the end, they die aboard and join the crew.</li>
<li><strong>The crew.</strong> Ghosts, delighted to have company after an eternity of the same faces. Some long to pass on. Some love their endless voyage. A few enjoy keeping guests forever.</li>
<li><strong>Tobiah Fennimore.</strong> Thirteen years ago Lord Xanthos sent his oldest colleague aboard to study the Heart. Tobiah never returned. He died in the flooded hold, and is now one of the crew.</li>

<p>Off a grey northern coast, where fishermen say the sea is “thin”, Lord Xanthos’s expedition ship, the <strong>Lantern of Silverwood</strong>, waits for dusk. At sunset the water begins to glow, a deep green light rising from far below, and the <strong>Saltgrave</strong> comes up out of it: a tall black ship, perfect in every rope and sail, with lanterns burning and a fiddle playing on her deck.</p>
<h3>Running it</h3>
<ul>
<li><strong>Six characters of level 5</strong>, 2024 rules. The level-5 pre-built characters are at the back.</li>
<li><strong>You need:</strong> the crew sheet (page 23), the map of the main deck (page 18) and Tobiah’s notes (the handout, page 25).</li>
<li><strong>Pacing:</strong> Act I about 55 minutes, Act II about 70, Act III about 70.</li>
</ul>
<h3>The night</h3>
<p>The night has three marks: <strong>dusk</strong> (Act I), <strong>midnight</strong> (Act II) and <strong>the last hours before dawn</strong> (Act III). Each big activity (a long game, the chart, the hold) takes an hour or two. Say aloud when the night moves on. It isn’t a tight timer, but <strong>at dawn the Saltgrave sails</strong>, with anyone still aboard.</p></div><div class="flow-col"><h3>Scaling</h3>

<h3>Playing Lord Xanthos</h3>
<p>A <strong>leader first and a wizard second</strong>, five or six levels above the party, grey now, measured and quietly formidable. Ask his name: “<strong>Lord Xanthos.</strong> Of Silverwood. I will be on this deck, and not on that one.”</p>
<ul>
<li><strong>Says he’s doing:</strong> finding out what became of Tobiah Fennimore.</li>
<li><strong>Privately:</strong> he wanted the Heart, to study. He still does, at the start.</li>
<li><strong>Why he won’t board:</strong> he sent a friend over that gangplank thirteen years ago and never saw him again. He is powerful, not foolish.</li>
<li><strong>What he does:</strong> holds the link between the two ships all night, and watches. When the binding breaks, the gangplank works, and he will be waiting at the other end of it. <strong>He doesn’t cross. He doesn’t pull anyone over by magic.</strong></li>

<p><em>About 55 minutes. Dusk.</em></p>
<h3>The hire</h3>
<p>Xanthos has hired the party through the Guild: <strong>200 gp each</strong>, and anything they can carry off the ship is theirs. On the deck of the Lantern, at dusk, he tells them:</p><blockquote data-label="READ ALOUD"><p><em>“Thirteen years ago I sent a friend across to that ship. Tobiah Fennimore: a careful man, a brilliant one. I have known him since we were both young.” Lord Xanthos watches the green glow rise. “He was to study a certain artefact aboard. He did not come back.</em></p>
<p><em>“I would like to know what happened to him. And I would very much like you to come back.”</em></p></blockquote><h3>The welcome</h3><blockquote data-label="READ ALOUD"><p><em>The Saltgrave slides alongside, close enough to touch. A gangplank thumps down between the ships. On her deck, a crowd of pale, grinning sailors, all of them faintly see-through, cheers as if you were long-lost family.</em></p>
<p><em>“LIVING ONES!” bellows a huge ghost with a ring through his nose. “Fresh faces! Fresh stories! Welcome aboard, ye living bastards! Somebody get them a drink!”</em></p></blockquote></div><div class="flow-col"><h3>Saul and Netta</h3>
<p>Two of Xanthos’s sailors, <strong>Saul</strong> (old, steady) and <strong>Netta</strong> (young, curious), carry the party’s gear across and help lash the gangplank. They step onto the Saltgrave’s deck too. <strong>They are trapped as well.</strong> In Act III, they need help getting off.</p>
<h3>The party aboard</h3>
<p>The ghosts are <strong>genuinely glad to see them</strong>. Most are not hostile: they want stories, games, wagers, songs and news. Let the players mingle. Use the crew sheet (page 23) for names and moods.</p>
<p><strong>Things to do:</strong>
- <strong>Dead Man’s Dice</strong> with Quartermaster Mags (she cheats; see Act II).
- <strong>A drinking song</strong> with the bosun, Big Hollin.
- <strong>Arm-wrestling</strong> a ghost (Athletics vs +5). Win and he’s your friend.
- <strong>Telling a story</strong> nobody has heard. They are starving for new ones. A good tale (DC 12 Performance, or no roll for a good effort) wins a friend.
- <strong>Ghost grog.</strong> Cold as the sea. Anyone who drinks it (DC 12 Con save) sees a <strong>shift into the past</strong>: the ship new and bright, the crew alive (see next page).</p></div></div></div><div class="folio-footer"><span>The Thirteenth Tide</span><span>5</span></div>

<div class="running" style="--accent:#2f5a5a"><span>The Thirteenth Tide</span><span>GM</span></div><div class="pf-body columns" style="--accent:#2f5a5a"><div class="layout-cols"><div class="flow-col"><h3>Brawls</h3>
<p>If a fight breaks out, ghosts can be struck down, but they <strong>reform a minute later</strong>, laughing. Nobody aboard truly dies again while the Heart is whole.</p>
<h3>Shifts into the past</h3>
<p>Now and then, without warning, the ship <strong>shifts</strong>: for a few heartbeats the deck is new, the sails white, the crew alive and sweating. Use these as clues:</p>
<ul>
<li><strong>Rook</strong> on the deck, triumphant, holding up a black pearl, crew cheering.</li>
<li><strong>A storm</strong> that came out of a clear sky, the night after.</li>
<li><strong>The sea turning green</strong>, and the ship sinking into it, not down but <strong>through</strong>.</li>
</ul></div><div class="flow-col"><h3>The gangplank</h3>
<p>At some point, <strong>someone tries to go back</strong>: to fetch gear, report to Xanthos, or just stretch their legs. Let it happen naturally. If nobody tries, Saul tries.</p><blockquote data-label="READ ALOUD"><p><em>You step onto the gangplank, walk three paces towards the Lantern, and…</em></p>
<p><em>You are standing on the Saltgrave’s deck again, facing the other way. The gangplank stretches out in front of you, empty.</em></p>
<p><em>On the Lantern’s rail, Lord Xanthos raises one grey eyebrow.</em></p>
<p><em>“Interesting,” he says.</em></p></blockquote><p>The ghosts <strong>roar with laughter</strong> and slap the character on the back. “<em>Nobody leaves, love. Not the living. Not ever. Didn’t anyone tell you?</em>”</p>
<p>Xanthos calls across: “<em>Do not panic. You have until dawn. I can hold the link until then. Find out how this works.</em>” He is calm. He is also, plainly, worried.</p>

<h3>Tobiah Fennimore</h3><figure class="tb-pic"><div class="tb-frame" style="height:220px"><img alt="The ghost of Tobiah Fennimore." src="https://oberonandomegaid.github.io/aelderia/oneshots/thirteenth-tide/img/tobiah.jpg" style="object-position:50% 20%"></div><figcaption>The ghost of Tobiah Fennimore.</figcaption></figure><blockquote data-label="READ ALOUD"><p><em>“I am sorry,” says the ghost, polishing spectacles that are no longer quite there. “I did not want to say anything until I was sure you were… well. He is calm, kind and sad. <strong>He helps, but he doesn’t know everything</strong>, and he won’t decide for them.</p></div><div class="flow-col"><h3>What Tobiah knows</h3>
<ul>
<li><strong>The Heart</strong> is a black pearl. It binds the soul of everyone who dies aboard, and keeps the ship from falling apart. It traps living guests.</li>
<li>He believes the only way to free everyone is to <strong>destroy it</strong>. “<em>I came here to bring it home. I was wrong. It cannot be carried off. It must be broken.</em>”</li>
<li>It is kept in the <strong>captain’s cabin</strong>, in a <strong>whalebone coffer</strong>. The key is lost in the <strong>flooded hold</strong>.</li>
<li>“<em>I went for the key,</em>” he says quietly. “<em>There is something down there. It doesn’t bother us. We have nothing it wants to eat.</em>” He looks at the party. “<em>You do.</em>”</li>
<li>Destroying it will <strong>sink the ship</strong>. “<em>She is very, very old. The Heart is all that holds her together.</em>”</li>
<li>He gives them <strong>his notes</strong> (the handout).</li>
</ul>
<h3>What he doesn't know</h3>
<ul>
<li>How to break the pearl. (The chart and Mags know.)</li>

All around her, faint shapes rise into the morning like steam off a kettle, and vanish.</em></p>
<p><em>The ghost of a man in spectacles stands at her rail, looking across at the Lantern. At the man on her deck.</em></p></blockquote><h3>Tobiah's farewell</h3><blockquote data-label="READ ALOUD"><p><em>“Xanthos,” calls Tobiah, and his voice is very faint. “I am sorry. I got careless. It was not your fault.”</em></p>
<p><em>Lord Xanthos stands very still at the rail. For a long moment he says nothing at all.</em></p>
<p><em>“Thirteen years,” he says at last. “You might have written.”</em></p>
<p><em>Tobiah laughs, and fades, and is gone.</em></p></blockquote></div><div class="flow-col"><h3>What happened</h3>
<ul>
<li><strong>The Heart is destroyed.</strong> Every soul aboard is free. <strong>Tobiah is gone</strong>, not brought back. He is at peace.</li>
<li><strong>The Saltgrave sinks</strong>, with almost all her gold. She will never return.</li>
<li><strong>Lord Xanthos</strong> got his answer, and lost his artefact. He looks at the place where the ship went down for a long time. Asked whether he minds: “<em>I came for Tobiah. The rest was curiosity.</em>” Whether that is the whole truth, he doesn’t say.</li>
<li><strong>Saul and Netta</strong>, if saved, buy the party drinks for the rest of their lives.</li>
</ul>
<h3>Rewards</h3>
<ul>
<li><strong>200 gp each</strong> from Xanthos, as promised.</li>
<li><strong>Whatever gold each character carried off</strong>: 250 gp per load.</li>
<li><strong>Mags’s loaded dice</strong>, if she gave them: they always roll a 6 on the first throw of any game, and never again that day.</li>
<li><strong>The story</strong>: the night the Saltgrave sank, which every tavern on the coast will want to hear.</li>

<h3>The fixed rules</h3>
<ul>
<li>No living creature can leave until the Heart is broken.</li>
<li>The Heart must be destroyed. Breaking it sinks the ship.</li>
<li>Tobiah is dead; he goes with the rest.</li>
<li>Xanthos never boards, and never rescues anyone by magic.</li>
<li>Dawn: the ship sails with anyone aboard.</li>
<li>Ghosts reform after a minute while the Heart is whole.</li>
</ul>
<h3>Breaking the Heart</h3>
<p>Key from the hold → coffer → iron from the land, wet with seawater. One hit.</p>

<p><strong>Notes on the Saltgrave. T. Fennimore.</strong></p>
<p><strong>Day 1.</strong> Boarded at dusk. A delightful crew. They have offered me a drink six times. I have accepted twice.</p>
<p><strong>Day 1, later.</strong> I cannot leave. I tried the gangplank. I am standing on the deck again. Most unsettling.</p>
<p><strong>The pearl.</strong> The captain calls it the Heart. Black, fist-sized, warm to look at. Kept in a whalebone coffer in his cabin. Key lost below. It binds the dead, and holds the ship together. I came to bring it home. <strong>I now believe it cannot be carried. It must be broken.</strong></p>
<p><strong>The hold.</strong> Flooded with another sea. Something large moves in it. It ignores the crew.</p>
<p><strong>Tonight</strong> I will try for the key.</p>
<p><em>The last line is smudged, as if written in a hurry, or in fear:</em></p>
<p>If anyone reads this, tell X I am sorry.</p></div></div><div class="oo-qr"><img alt="QR code" src="https://oberonandomegaid.github.io/aelderia/oneshots/thirteenth-tide/qr/handout-1.png"><span><b>SCAN ME</b>Open “Tobiah’s notes”<br>on your phone</span></div></div><div class="folio-footer"><span>The Thirteenth Tide</span><span>25</span></div>
